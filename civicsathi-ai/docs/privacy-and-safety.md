# Privacy and safety

- Do not enter sensitive personal or account information into this prototype.
- Avoid logging raw complaint text; audit events should be minimal.
- AI outputs are drafts and must be reviewed by a resident.
- Duplicate similarity is a hint only and must not cause automatic rejection.
- Live complaint submission, payment, approval, case closure and private-record access are not implemented.
- The service catalogue is illustrative until verified against current official public information.
